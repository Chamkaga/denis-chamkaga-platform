# Denis Chamkaga Platform – Visitor & Client User Manual

Welcome to the **Denis Chamkaga Platform** user guide.

---

## 1. Navigating Public Services

- **Homepage (`/`)**: Explore Denis's core expertise, client testimonials, target industries, and live contact widgets.
- **Portfolio (`/projects`)**: Browse completed business projects (Retail POS, Agribusiness, E-Commerce, Custom CRM) filtered by category and tech stack.
- **Services (`/services`)**: Review technical offerings, database optimization services, and consultation packages.
- **Business Checker (`/business-checker`)**: Run a digital readiness audit for your retail or enterprise business to detect inventory leakage and workflow bottlenecks.

---

## 2. Interacting with Mary AI

1. Click the **Mary AI** widget button at the bottom-right corner of any page.
2. Ask questions in **English** or **Swahili** regarding services, pricing, project timelines, or database architecture.
3. Complete lead qualification prompts (select business type, budget bracket, timeline).
4. Request a formal quotation or strategy consultation directly through Mary.

---

## 3. Viewing Quotations & Invoices

- **Quotation Viewer (`/public/quotation/:token`)**: View itemized project milestones, subtotal, and tax breakdown. Click **Approve Proposal** or **Request Revision**.
- **Invoice Checkout (`/public/invoice/:token`)**: Pay securely via M-Pesa, Tigopesa, Bank Transfer, or Credit Card powered by Flutterwave.
